# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

### End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python • PostgreSQL • Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using a dataset of 3,900 transactions to uncover purchasing patterns, customer preferences, spending habits, and subscription trends.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, starting with data cleaning and exploratory data analysis in Python, followed by SQL analysis in PostgreSQL, and ending with an interactive Power BI dashboard and business recommendations.

**Objective:** Transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support marketing, customer retention, and revenue optimization.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 records and 18 columns**, covering:

- **Customer Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status
- **Purchase Information:** Product, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color
- **Shopping Behavior:** Discounts, Promo Codes, Previous Purchases, Purchase Frequency
- **Customer Experience:** Review Ratings and Shipping Preferences

**Data Quality:** 37 missing values were identified in the Review Rating column.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

**1. Data Loading and Exploration**

- Imported the dataset using Python and Pandas.
- Examined data types, structure, and descriptive statistics.
- Identified missing values and potential inconsistencies.

**2. Data Cleaning and Preparation**

- Filled 37 missing Review Rating values using category-specific median ratings.
- Standardized column names using snake_case.
- Created new features such as `age_group` and `purchase_frequency_days`.
- Identified redundant information and removed the `promo_code_used` column.
- Loaded the cleaned data into PostgreSQL.

**3. SQL Business Analysis**

Used PostgreSQL to investigate ten business questions:

1. Revenue generated by male and female customers.
2. High-spending customers who use discounts.
3. Top five products based on customer ratings.
4. Average purchase value by shipping type.
5. Spending differences between subscribers and non-subscribers.
6. Products most dependent on discounts.
7. Customer segmentation into New, Returning, and Loyal groups.
8. Top three best-selling products in each category.
9. Relationship between repeat purchases and subscription status.
10. Revenue contribution across customer age groups.

SQL techniques included `JOIN`, `GROUP BY`, aggregate functions, `CASE WHEN`, CTEs, and window functions.

**4. Power BI Dashboard**

Developed an interactive dashboard to visualize key performance indicators and customer behavior.

**5. Business Reporting**

Summarized the analytical findings and developed data-driven business recommendations.

### 🔍 Key Insights

**1. Subscription Opportunities**

- Only 27% of records belong to subscribed customers.
- Subscribers and non-subscribers have similar average purchase amounts: $59.49 and $59.87, respectively.
- Improving subscription benefits may help increase customer participation.

**2. Customer Loyalty**

- The Loyal segment represents approximately 80% of the dataset.
- Returning customers represent approximately 18%.
- This segmentation highlights opportunities for loyalty campaigns and targeted retention strategies.

**3. Gender-Based Revenue**

- Male customers generated $157,890 in revenue.
- Female customers generated $75,191.
- These differences can help guide customer segmentation and marketing analysis.

**4. Product Performance**

The highest-rated products include:

| Product | Average Rating |
|---------|---------------|
| Gloves | 3.86 |
| Sandals | 3.84 |
| Boots | 3.82 |
| Hat | 3.80 |
| Skirt | 3.78 |

**5. Shipping Preferences**

- Express shipping: $60.48 average purchase amount.
- Standard shipping: $58.46 average purchase amount.
- Express shipping orders have a slightly higher average purchase value.

**6. Discount Behavior**

- Hats, sneakers, coats, sweaters, and pants recorded some of the highest discount usage rates.
- Hats had a 50% discount usage rate.
- These findings can support more targeted promotional strategies.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

**1. Improve Subscription Adoption**

Introduce exclusive offers and benefits to encourage non-subscribers to join.

**2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty**

Develop rewards programs for repeat customers and personalized campaigns for returning shoppers.

**3. Optimize Discount Strategies**

Review products with high discount usage to balance sales growth with profitability.

**4. Promote High-Performing Products**

Feature highly rated and frequently purchased products in marketing campaigns.

**5. Implement Targeted Marketing**

Use customer segmentation and age-group revenue analysis to create more relevant marketing campaigns.
